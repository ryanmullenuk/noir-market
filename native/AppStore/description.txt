Trade smart. Play dirty. Come out on top.

NOIR MARKET is a dark, darkly funny strategy game about risk, rumour and very bad decisions. Buy low, sell high and stay one step ahead of the police, the loan sharks and the local crews across a fictional criminal underworld spanning the UK and Ireland. How much can you be worth when the thirty days are up?

READ THE MARKET
Every city has its own cheap goods and its own big demand. Prices move every night, big orders move the market against you, and festival weekends, match days and weather change everything. Learn the patterns, plan your route and time your moves.

TRUST NO ONE
Every morning the NOIRNET wire comes through: real price shocks, local gossip and intel that's true exactly half the time. Pay informants for tips, build their loyalty, or stiff them and find out what happens when a snitch holds a grudge. Burner phones bring off-the-books deals. Some are gold. Some are a man in a van who never turns up.

SURVIVE THE STREETS
Police stops, customs at the airport, debt collectors with torches and gangs under the street lamp. Every encounter is a choice with visible odds: bribe, run, bargain, fight or go quietly. Sit in one city too long and a Breaking News tip-off warns you a raid is coming: stash everything in your lock-up, get out of town, or chance it. And the more money you walk around with, the bigger the gangs that come looking.

BUILD AN EMPIRE
Borrow from five lenders who each have their own way of saying "pay me back". Ship crates ahead overnight. Buy a kebab shop, a laundrette or a taxi firm to wash your money. Hire a driver, a lookout or some muscle. Take delivery contracts with hard deadlines. Earn respect, because respect means better prices, more work and fewer people trying to rob you.

CHOOSE YOUR ENDING
On the last night, retire to Spain, risk it all on one last score, or simply walk away. If the heat is on, the police might get there first.

MODES AND PROGRESSION
